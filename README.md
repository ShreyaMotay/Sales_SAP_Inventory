# SAP Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project simulates an SAP Sales & Inventory reporting environment by analyzing sales and inventory data using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into revenue performance, inventory utilization, product trends, and regional sales distribution to support business decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations using SAP ERP systems generate large volumes of sales and inventory data through modules such as:

- SAP SD (Sales & Distribution)
- SAP MM (Materials Management)

Business leaders require actionable insights into product performance, inventory levels, and sales trends to improve operational efficiency and profitability.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and inventory performance
- Monitor revenue and product trends
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Track inventory utilization and stock levels
- Develop an interactive dashboard for decision-making

---

## Key Insights

- Technology products generated the highest revenue.
- The West region contributed the largest share of total sales.
- High inventory utilization was observed among top-selling products.
- Revenue concentration was strongest within the Technology category.
- Inventory monitoring helped identify potential stock shortages.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory replenishment for high-demand products.
- Increase focus on high-performing regions to maximize revenue.
- Monitor low-stock items proactively to prevent stockouts.
- Leverage product performance insights for strategic planning.
